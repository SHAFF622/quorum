Is this research finding real?

AI scientists produce findings faster than experts can check them, and about 1 in 5 is wrong. Before anyone spends months in a lab on a finding, check it here: Quorum's referees re-test it on thousands of real breast cancer patients in seconds.

Who it's forResearchers and clinical teams who use AI to study patient data

Cancer researchers, hospital data teams, and pharma or biotech groups running AI scientists like Kosmos.

The problemAI findings can be confidently wrong

The best AI scientist is wrong about 1 in 5 times, and 2 in 5 when it explains results. Checking every claim by hand takes an expert days.

How Quorum solves itIndependent referees re-check every finding in seconds

Each one recomputes the finding from the raw data in a different way. Every finding gets a card with the reason in plain words.
