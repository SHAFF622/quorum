The problem

AI can now write research findings in minutes. Nobody can check them that fast.

AI scientists read thousands of papers and analyze patient data on their own. The best one, Kosmos, is wrong about 1 in 5 times overall, and wrong about 2 in 5 times when it explains what a result means. Today a trained expert re-checks every claim by hand, about 25 claims per discovery, before anyone acts on it.

Quorum does that first check automatically. Independent referees re-check each claim against the raw data, and every claim gets a card a non-expert can read:

Who it helps

Research teams using AI scientists, who lose weeks chasing findings that were never real.

Hospitals and clinics with patient data but no statistician on staff: upload a spreadsheet, get a checked answer.

Patients, because fewer false leads reach the lab.

Why it scales

It checks any AI scientist's claims, on any survival dataset, in about a minute, for cents instead of expert-days.
